About

Puggle Monster Studios is a small independent studio in Western New York.

We build focused apps that do one job well, keep your data on your own device wherever we can, and skip the ads and trackers.

Why “Puggle Monster”?

The studio is named for Jackson, our late puggle, who answered to “Puggle Monster” and ran the house with a wagging tail. His curiosity and stubborn cheer are what we hope people feel when they open one of our apps.

The company

Puggle Monster Studios apps are made by NORTH & MAIN STUDIOS INC., a New York corporation.
