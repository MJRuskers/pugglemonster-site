Pete's Bathroom Rankings Support

Need help with Pete's Bathroom Rankings? We’re a small studio and we read every email.

Contact

Email support@pugglemonster.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please tell us your device model, OS version and the app version (Settings / About).

Common questions

  • Do I have to sign in? No. Sign-in is optional (Google or X). Rankings/listings on the server stay public either way.
  • Are my ratings private? No. Restroom listings and ratings are public server data, not only on your phone.
  • Why does the app use my location? Pin city lookup can send the pin’s coordinates to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to name the place. It isn’t used for ads or cross-app tracking.
  • How do I delete my account? Email support@pugglemonster.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. Say if you also want specific ratings removed.
  • Do you use analytics like PostHog? No.
  • Refunds: App Store purchases go through Apple. Use reportaproblem.apple.com.
