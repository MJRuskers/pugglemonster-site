Pete’s Bathroom Rankings Support

Need help with Pete’s Bathroom Rankings? We’re a small studio and we read every email.

Contact

Email support@pugglemonster.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please tell us your device model, OS version and the app version (Settings or About).

Common questions

  • Where are my rankings saved? On your device only. There is no account or cloud backup, so deleting the app (or clearing its data) deletes them.
  • How do I erase everything? Delete the app (or, on the web, clear this site’s data).
  • Does the app collect any data? No. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Does it work offline? Yes.
  • Sharing: If you share from the app, it uses your device’s share sheet. We don’t see recipients.
  • Refunds: App Store purchases go through Apple. Use reportaproblem.apple.com.
