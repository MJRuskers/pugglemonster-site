Pete's Bathroom Rankings Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Short version: Pete's Bathroom Rankings collects no data. Nothing leaves your device: no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your rankings stay on this phone.

Who we are

Pete's Bathroom Rankings is made by HUMAN RESOURCES ON DEMAND INC., a New York corporation (“we”).

What stays on your device

Your rankings, scores, pins and settings are saved on this device only (in the app’s local storage). An optional backup/restore file you export stays wherever you save it; we never see it. Sharing uses your device’s share sheet, so we don’t see what you share or who you share it with.

No data collected

Pete's Bathroom Rankings has no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising, and it doesn’t send rankings to us. We can’t see how you use the app.

Sign-in

Some builds include an optional sign-in. Your rankings still stay on this device. If you use sign-in, only the sign-in details needed to keep you signed in are used for that purpose — and nothing about your rankings is uploaded with them. If that ever changes, we’ll update this page first.

What we don’t do

No analytics, no advertising, no selling or sharing personal information, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no data brokers. We don’t collect personal data that identifies you from your rankings.

Children

Pete's Bathroom Rankings is a general-audience app. It isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. The app collects no rankings data from anyone.

Your choices and rights

Everything the app stores is on your device. Delete the app or clear this site’s data to erase it; delete any backup files you saved yourself. We hold no rankings data about you. If you email us at support@pugglemonster.com, we use your email only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and the app’s listing.
