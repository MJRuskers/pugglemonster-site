Pete’s Bathroom Rankings Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Short version: Pete’s Bathroom Rankings collects no data. Nothing leaves your device: no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your rankings and reviews stay on your device only.

Who we are

Pete’s Bathroom Rankings is made by HUMAN RESOURCES ON DEMAND INC., a New York corporation (“we”).

What stays on your device

Pete’s Bathroom Rankings saves its data on your device only: the bathrooms you rank, your scores and short reviews, and your settings. We never upload any of it.

No data collected

Pete’s Bathroom Rankings has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no sign-in, and it doesn’t send anything to us or to anyone else. We can’t see how you use the app. If that changes in a future version, we’ll update this policy and the app’s App Store privacy label.

Sharing

If you share something from the app, it goes through your device’s share sheet. We don’t see what you share or who you share it with.

Deleting your data

To erase everything, delete the app, or on the web clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

What we don’t do

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no selling or sharing personal information, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no data brokers. We don’t collect personal data that identifies you.

Children

Pete’s Bathroom Rankings is a general-audience app. It isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. The app collects no data from anyone.

Your choices and rights

Everything the app stores is on your device, and you can delete it by deleting the app or clearing its data. We hold no data about you. If you email us at support@pugglemonster.com, we use your email only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and the app’s listing.
