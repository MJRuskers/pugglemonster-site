Privacy

Effective October 17, 2026

Short version: our apps are built to keep your information on your own device. We don’t sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking on this website.

What our apps do

  • Most data you enter (rosters, lists, notes, settings) is stored in your browser or on your device. You can delete it any time by clearing the app’s data.
  • We don’t require accounts unless an app clearly says so, and we never sell or rent personal information.
  • When an app sends a text or email, it opens your own messaging app; we don’t see the message.
  • Lantern Keys, Flamblings, Ms. White Flamingo, Captain Ops and Rec League Warrior collect anonymous usage data with PostHog, which you can turn off in the app (“Share anonymous usage data”). See each app’s policy.
  • The web versions are hosted on Grok App Builder, which may load its own scripts. Those are the host’s, not analytics run by us.
  • Our apps are not medical, legal or financial advice, and aren’t meant for children under 13.
