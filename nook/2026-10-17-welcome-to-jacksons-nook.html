Welcome to Jackson’s Nook

October 17, 2026

Jackson, our late puggle, was the original Puggle Monster and the reason this studio has its name. His favorite spot was the nook of our bent legs, where he loved to curl up and cuddle. We called it Jackson’s nook, and it felt like the right name for a cozy corner of this site.

What you’ll find here

This is where we’ll post notes on new apps and updates as we build them: what changed, what got easier, and what we’re working on. Posts will be short and plain. You can read them here, or follow along with the Atom feed.

Small apps with a big heart

Puggle Monster Studios makes small apps that each do one job well. We keep your information on your own device wherever we can, and we don’t add ads or analytics. You can see everything we make on the Apps page.

Thanks for stopping by. Questions or ideas are always welcome at support@pugglemonster.com.
