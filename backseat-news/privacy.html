Backseat News Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Short version: Backseat News collects no data. Nothing leaves your device: no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Junior Reporter stories stay on your device only.

Who we are

Backseat News is made by HUMAN RESOURCES ON DEMAND INC., a New York corporation (“we”).

What stays on your device

Backseat News is an offline-first kids’ newspaper story maker. It saves its data on your device only: the stories you build from modular editions, your progress and your settings. There is no free-text chat with us, and we never upload any of it.

No data collected

Backseat News has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no sign-in, and it doesn’t send anything to us or to anyone else. We can’t see how you use the app.

Sharing and parent lock

Sharing uses your device’s share sheet, and a parent lock / family code may gate sharing so a grown-up can decide when something leaves the device. We don’t see what you share or who you share it with.

Deleting your data

To erase everything, delete the app, or on the web clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

What we don’t do

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no selling or sharing personal information, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no data brokers. We don’t collect personal data that identifies you.

Children

Backseat News is made for kids (about age 4 and up) with a parent nearby. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. The app collects no data from anyone.

Your choices and rights

Everything the app stores is on your device, and you can delete it by deleting the app or clearing its data. We hold no data about you. If you email us at support@pugglemonster.com, we use your email only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and the app’s listing.
