Contact

Email support@pugglemonster.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please tell us your device model, OS version and the app version (More → About).

Common questions

  • Where is my stuff saved? On your device only. There is no account or cloud backup, so deleting the app (or clearing its data) deletes it.
  • How do I turn off analytics? More → Settings → “Share anonymous usage data”.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. Analytics just isn’t sent until you’re back online.
  • Refunds: App Store purchases go through Apple. Use reportaproblem.apple.com.
